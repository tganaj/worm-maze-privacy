# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "WormMaze" ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our application on Apple iOS and iPadOS.

## Summary

WormMaze does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data of its own. The App works offline except for one optional, user-initiated feature: watching a rewarded video ad to refill a life, which uses Google AdMob. The App has no account, analytics, or other online features.

## Information We Collect

**None.** The App does not collect any personal information, from you or automatically.

## Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves your game progress locally, in its own app sandbox:

- Current level, maze layout, score, and unlocked levels
- Selected language

This data:
- never leaves your device,
- is never transmitted to us or to any third party,
- is removed automatically when you delete the App.

## Third-Party Services

The App's optional "watch an ad for a life" button uses Google AdMob (part of Google's Mobile Ads SDK) to show a rewarded video ad - only when you tap it, never automatically. Showing an ad lets Google and its advertising partners process technical and advertising identifiers and serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the App first asks Google's User Messaging Platform to collect your consent before showing an ad; if consent is declined or unavailable, only non-personalized ads are shown.

Other than that, the App does not integrate Game Center, any other advertising network, analytics SDK, crash reporter, or any other third-party service.

## App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

The App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or any other cross-app/cross-site identifier - ads shown are non-personalized.

## Permissions and Device Access

The App does not request any iOS permissions or device capabilities:

- Other than loading the optional rewarded ad described above, no network access is used for gameplay (the one other exception is the optional "more games" links in the menu, below)
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
- No Game Center, push notifications, or in-app purchases

## Links to Other Apps

The App's menu contains optional links to the developer's other apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Tapping one opens your browser or the respective store app, which is governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, not by this one.

## Children's Privacy

The App itself does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13, and is suitable for a general audience. The optional rewarded-ad feature described above is not intended to target children.

## Your Rights

Since the App collects nothing, there is no data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. Deleting the App removes all of its locally stored data (progress, settings) from your device.

## Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is processed, this policy will be updated and the new version published at the same address, with an updated "Last updated" date above.
