# Privacy Policy — Samsung Galaxy Store edition

**Effective date:** November 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the WormMaze app ("the app") handles user information when distributed through **Samsung Galaxy Store**.

## Summary

WormMaze does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data of its own. The app works offline except for one optional, user-initiated feature: watching a rewarded video ad to refill a life, which uses Google AdMob.

## What data the app processes

The app stores only your game progress (current level, maze, score, unlocked levels, lives, and language setting) locally on your device, in the app's private storage. This data:

- never leaves your device,
- is never sent to the developer or to any third party,
- is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising

The app's "watch an ad for a life" button is optional and only runs when you tap it. It uses Google AdMob (part of Google's Mobile Ads SDK) to show a rewarded video ad. Showing an ad lets Google and its advertising partners process technical and advertising identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) and serve ads, including measuring ad performance, under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Mobile Ads is provided through Google Play Services, which is preinstalled on Samsung Galaxy devices even when the app itself is installed from Galaxy Store rather than Google Play.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app first asks Google's User Messaging Platform to collect your consent before showing an ad; if consent is declined or unavailable, only non-personalized ads are shown.

This app does not integrate any other advertising or analytics SDK, and the ad only appears when you choose to watch one - it is never shown automatically.

## Samsung Galaxy Store

The app is distributed through Samsung Galaxy Store. Samsung Electronics processes your installation and update requests according to its own terms. We do not receive personal information from Samsung Galaxy Store about who downloaded the app. See [Samsung's Privacy Policy](https://www.samsung.com/global/privacy/).

## What the app does not do

- It does not use any analytics or tracking tools of its own (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics).
- It does not require registration, sign-in, or an account.
- It does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
- It has no in-app purchases or billing of any kind.
- Other than loading the optional rewarded ad described above, it does not connect to the internet for gameplay purposes (see the one other exception below).
- It does not integrate Samsung Account or Samsung Health.

## Links to app stores

The app's menu contains optional links to the developer's other apps on Google Play and the App Store. Opening them redirects to your web browser or the respective store app, which is governed by Google's or Apple's own privacy policy, not by this one.

## Children

The app itself does not collect any personal data from any user, including children. The optional rewarded-ad feature described above is subject to Google AdMob's own handling of child-directed treatment and is not intended to target children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is processed, this policy will be updated and the new version published at the same address.
